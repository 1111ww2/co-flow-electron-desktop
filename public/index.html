Privacy logic

Camera detection runs locally. Co-Flow does not record, store, or upload video. The prototype only calculates movement level and inactive time to update energy value.

  • Low movement increases inactive time.
  • Clear movement resets inactivity thresholds.
  • Energy decreases only when a threshold is reached.
  • Prototype mode counts low movement quickly for demo.
